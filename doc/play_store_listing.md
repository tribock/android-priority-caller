# Google Play Store listing

## Short description (max 80 characters)

Priority contacts ring loud and break through Do Not Disturb automatically

## Short description

Using Android's built-in Call Screening feature, Priority Caller quietly checks every incoming call against your saved list. If the caller isn't on your list, nothing happens — the app stays out of the way and your normal ringtone and settings apply. If the caller IS on your list, the app plays a loud ringtone on the alarm audio stream, the same way alarm clocks aren't silenced by Do Not Disturb, so you hear it no matter what.
